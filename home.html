Who We Are

We know the road because we walk it

Brain Injury Matters exists for the moment after the hospital doors close. We are a charity built on lived experience and professional expertise, fighting for a life not defined by injury.

  • Over 20 years of trusted support in the UK
  • A team that listens before it acts
  • Safe, transparent, and community-led
